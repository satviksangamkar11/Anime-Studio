- [Verified Fact] Should You Stack Any LoRA? (And Existence of 2-, 4-, and 8-Step HD Variants):
  - Never stack a separate Turbo LoRA on top of official pika_h3_flex_{fl2va,ref2va}_v1_bf16.safetensors (1.96 GB): Official Pika H3 Flex is already trajectory-distilled to 6 steps (ManualSigmas: 1.0, 0.972973, 0.923077, 0.8, 0.571429, 0.333333, 0.0).   - Yes—Use the Pre-Fused Pika-H3-Flex-Turbo-LoRA (2-, 4-, and 8-Step HD Variants) on Singularity v2: The 8-step HD option you heard about is real and lives in Pika-Labs/Pika-H3-Flex-Turbo-LoRA (committed October 29–30, 2026 by akhaliq, commits 3ef4785–dcacd93). Instead of naive additive stacking, it attenuates Pika Flex to its few-step operating point (s = 0.5 for 2/4-step; s = 0.6 for 8-step HD) and fuses it with lightx2v / pDMD turbo weights (1.0) via exact per-layer rank concatenation (B_fused A_fused = s B_flex A_flex + 1.0 B_turbo A_turbo, 3.91 GB full or 0.31 GB rank-32 SVD lite_r32). Running pika_h3_flex_turbo_8step_hd_ref2va.safetensors at 8 steps (simple scheduler, CFG 1.0, strength 1.0) on Singularity v2 Ref2VA gives you Singularity v2's HDR face restoration plus Pika Flex's prompt obedience and lip-sync in one pass (Pika-Labs/Pika-H3-Flex-Turbo-LoRA, October 30, 2026; RunComfy 8-Step Ref2Video Workflow, November 4, 2026).
- [Verified Fact] Reconciling the Singularity v2 Ref2VA Settings Discrepancy:
  - Why SamplerCustomAdvanced (not KSampler) is required: MiniMax H3 denoises a packed NestedTensor combining a 24-channel video latent (video_shift = 12.0) and a 32-channel stereo audio latent (audio_shift = 3.0) at CFG 1.0 (ComfyUI Issue #15250, August 3, 2026). Standard KSampler lacks dual-clock AV shift routing (BasicGuider + KSamplerSelect + BasicScheduler → SamplerCustomAdvanced).
  - Why the community transcript uses euler + beta, 10 steps (with 4-step LoRA at 0.9): Because Singularity v2's base weights are un-distilled, running a 4-step Turbo LoRA (minimax_h3_ref2v_turbo_4step_v0.1) at 4 steps + simple softens facial details and drifts on multi-character sheets (Stable Diffusion Art Ref2VA Guide, October 19, 2026).   - Why the pruned HF model card uses 6 steps (euler + simple, 4-step LoRA at 0.7): On WAS/Minimax-h3_Singularity_v2_pruned (October 21–26, 2026), 6 steps + euler + simple with the DMAD 4-step LoRA at 0.7 is the standardized regression benchmark used to verify PSNR (26.1 dB on r2 INT8) against the 66.3 GB BF16 checkpoint while avoiding audio crackle that beta can cause at very low step counts (Reddit r/StableDiffusion Step/Audio Analysis, August 5, 2026).
