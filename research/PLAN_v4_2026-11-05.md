## Claude's amendments
1. **H3 Control speed:** do not assume 20-40 steps. Test Control with a generic turbo LoRA (TaoMate 3-step or Larry 4-step, euler) against 20 steps. If the turbo works, Control costs about the same as Pika.
2. **Cheap speed check on the baseline:** TaoMate FL2VA 3-step vs Pika FL2VA 6-step on the same keyframe (could halve the cost of every H3-FL shot).
3. **Licence is gate 0 for publishing, not for testing:** the H3 licence (primary text, read 2026-11-05) excludes the EU, UK, South Korea and US and covers outputs. Because Pika and Singularity inherit it, LTX-2.5 Layout to Render and Wan Animate 2 matter as non-H3 routes. Test them even if H3 Control works.
4. **Blender is code: write it with the strong models.** A `blender_layout.py` (bpy) generator that, from a shot's layout block, builds scale-correct proxies (child Krishna about 5.5 heads; Kaliya as a spline-IK serpent with a fan of hoods), places the set, animates key poses and the camera, and renders playblast + depth + pose passes at the shot's exact frame count. Write it with Opus or Kimi 3.1 Pro (owner: available for coding). Until 02:00 tonight, agents are Haiku medium only.
5. **Close-up mouth animation needs a still head:** Flow mouth-shape edits of one approved close-up, masked into the mouth area only. If the head moves, use H3 soundtrack mode or cut.
6. **One Kaggle session = one model group:** queue all H3-FL/H3-REF shots together, Control shots together, LTX shots together; a session never switches model families.
7. **Proxy-first, mesh-later:** no Pixal3D/Hunyuan3D meshes until the proxies prove insufficient (agrees with the audit).
