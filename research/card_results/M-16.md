## Why writer_seconds is the "edit seconds"
The lock always sizes a voiced shot to its voice (free cut = need), so lock edit_frames >= voice by construction.
The only real fixed length is the writer's own writer_seconds in the blocking sheet (BLK-S13). The check is against that.

## Hand-computed values
- en 2.40 s, hi 4.00 s, narration from 0.5 s: hi need = 0.5 + 4.0 + 0.1 = 4.60 s; edit 3.00 s -> refused.
- hi 2.50 s: need 3.10 s = 74.4 frames -> 75 frames; edit 3.10 s passes.
- 80-frame edit from 107 pixel frames: trim span 80, tail handle 107 - 80 = 27.
