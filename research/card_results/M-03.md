# M-03 time-pointed stages, one camera sentence, hold wording (Sonnet 5.5, 2026-11-12)

## Built
- h3/v4_to_h3.py: `_stages(..., beats, hold_names)`: every stage starts with ONE time point ("At second N," stages 1-6,
  "By second N," stage 7); N is a half-second tick, strictly increasing, from the span start or from a blocking beat that
  falls inside the span (beat t is editorial time, head handle added). An empty stage emits "<name> does not move." for each
  held cast member (cast hold flags; none flagged = whole cast; no cast = "the scene holds still"). The placeholder
  "The figure holds still, part N of 7." is gone. `_camera_from_sheet`: for blocking/1.1 sheets exactly one camera sentence
  from the camera.move enum + ", because <motivation>", or "The camera stays locked"; shake as "; a short shake at second N".
  blocking/1.0 sheets and packs without a sheet keep the old `_camera`. `_chain_header`: chain_from -> cast_lock.chain_header
  = director/chain_step.chain_header(state), state = sheet.chain_state, else last beat action of the chained-from sheet,
  else a problem. `_lint_sheets` lints a chained sheet together with its predecessor (L17 needed it; only that sheet's
  messages are kept).
- h3/compile_h3_sing8.py: `_fl2va` puts the header right after the style-prefix sentence when cast_lock.chain_header is set.
- h3/schema_and_linter.py: CAMERA_MOVE_CLAUSES table; extra official phrases (pulls back, rises slowly, descends slowly,
  circles, stays locked, drifts very slightly); CM-05 (exactly one camera sentence), CM-06 (shake wording), TP-02 (time range);
  `lint_story(..., routes=None)`: an S row chosen only by the routes file is linted start-only (copy without picture_2).
  TP-01 (no time point at all) deliberately NOT a rule: old stories and the FL2VA golden have none.
- director/shot_router.py `plan_from_blocking`: a sheet with chain_from gets route S (start-only, no B); with the S gate off it
  raises RouterError (a continuation has no end picture to fall back to).
- h3/tests/test_opus_examples.py (new, 14 tests): the five Opus examples compiled from blocking/1.1 sheets and the unit rules.

## Not changed

- FL2VA/S goldens: unchanged (hash 3b99fa5f... still passes). No old test needed editing, so rule 58(5) list: none.
