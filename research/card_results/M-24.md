## Added
- `director/keyframe_gate.py`
  - `KEYFRAME_MIN_SSIM = 0.80` passed to `background_lock` (was the 0.97 default).
  - `KEYFRAME_MAX_DE2000 = 6.0` (core g1). `palette_de2000` / `palette_ok`: dE2000 of mean Lab of background pixels
    (outside both masks), reusing `core.style_pack.srgb_to_lab` and `delta_e_2000`. Strided sample of 2000 pixels.
  - `gate()` records `palette_de2000` and `palette_ok`; `palette_ok` is part of the pass.
- `director/build_h3_batch.py`
  - `add_settings_sha(manifest, script, pin=WAN2GP_PIN)` now also writes `render_version` per shot and for the batch:
    sha256 over {Wan2GP pin, `h3_director.PRESET` hash, the settings (steps, cfg, sampler, seed, resolution, prompt, A/B image paths)}.
- `h3/render_controller.py`
  - `MAX_RENDERS_PER_SHOT = 3`, `render_allowed(history, shot_id, owner_flag=False)`: 4th render refused without owner flag.
  - `DRIFT_ORDER` (simplify, remove_camera_move, move_b_closer, split_or_chain, new_seed, route_change) and
    `record_drift_step(log, step)`: strictly increasing, unknown or repeated steps refused.
