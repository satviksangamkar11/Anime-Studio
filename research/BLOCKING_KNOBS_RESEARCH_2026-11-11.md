# Blocking lint knobs and the "turning an object" functionality: research (2026-11-11, Haiku medium, offline transcripts + web; condensed)

Scope: no official source (MiniMax/Hailuo H3, fal.ai, Kapwing, Wan FLF2V pages) gives a number for any of the four knobs; everything numeric below is inference unless marked as a source. No local render log measured them either.

## A. The four knobs
1. MAX_SPEED_H = 0.25 frame-heights per second.
 - Local (qualitative): aininja94 "Motion Dial" video [06:23]-[06:42] (fast characters smear and blur); PGP4Hrir6hk [10:38]-[11:23] (fast continuous action makes hair/body edges soft; use a normal pace); stRsg-wNQds [14:33]-[17:20] (high motion dial gave "zoomies", running at moderate values was acceptable); jBrsQZVPLU4 [10:30] (problem with fast motions). Web: fal.ai H3 guide and Kapwing H3 guide: very fast movement gives the model less room to resolve clean geometry (no number).
 - Sanity check (inference): average adult walk about 1.31 m/s (2021 meta-analysis, 35 studies) / FHWA 1.2 m/s; a frame-height is about 2.5-3 m in a medium-full shot and about 5 m in a wide shot, so a normal walk is about 0.43-0.52 FH/s (medium-full) and about 0.26 FH/s (wide). A full cross of the 9:16 frame width (0.5625 FH) in 1 s is 0.56 FH/s.
 - Reading: 0.25 is about half a normal walk in a medium-full frame, so the lint would reject an ordinary walk-across in a 3-5 s shot. Researcher recommends 0.5 FH/s as the default ceiling for walking and 0.25 only as an explicit slow/drift class; low confidence; calibrate with the first real render.
2. POSE_CHANGE_MIN_SEC = 1.0: no evidence found. A real sit-to-stand takes about 1 s; a generative model morphs more with larger gaps, so the true minimum may be larger. Suggestion: 1.0 s sit<->stand, 1.5 s kneel/lie, 2.0 s lying<->standing (low confidence).
3. CONT_TOL = 0.05: local evidence is qualitative only (MISTAKES F2/F3: separately generated A and B disagreed; rule: B is an edit of A; UNIFIED_pipeline_from_two_videos_2026-11-05.md:17,53: chain policy carries about 22 motion frames, planned checks frame-0 match, face match, motion jump, colour drift, no numbers). fal.ai: condition the next clip on the ending state of the previous. 0.05 FH is about 9% of frame width: a placeholder; add pose and facing match, not position alone (low confidence).
4. MAX_MOVING = 3: no stated limit anywhere (Pika H3 Flex up to 12 reference inputs; "two or three characters" in one reference). The limit is probably identity drift and face size. Keep 3 as provisional; measure two vs three movers in a real render (low confidence).

## B. "Turning an object"
Facts, saved videos: xwTJ3E3NU7A [05:06]-[05:29]: a separate TURNAROUND workflow (needs one extra LoRA; input = a character or asset image + short description; output = a turntable video plus an eight-view sheet): an asset workflow, not a shot generator. Same video [10:11]-[10:27]: a specific camera move was built in Blender and loaded as a 3D reference because prompting alone could not do it (a camera orbit, not a character turn). aininja94 Motion Dial [03:01],[05:20]: a small LoRA-like safetensor that changes how much motion the model adds, not facing. PGP4Hrir6hk [10:07]-[10:25]: a six-panel storyboard drives camera moves and cuts; turns and arm swings rendered well.
Facts, web: Kapwing H3 guide: a timed subject turn is written in the timeline, e.g. "The model performs a slow quarter turn to the right" at [3-6s]; product template "rotates approximately 30 degrees"; "Orbit" is a CAMERA option. fal.ai H3 page: "full 360-degree product reveal" as a prompt phrase; wide shots: use back view / rear three-quarter view. Community (Scenario, Reddit; not official): a 360 must be asked for explicitly and the prompt should say the end pose matches the start; arcs stop part way unless the full turn is named; plain backgrounds help; hit-or-miss.
Reading (inference): "turning" means one of three things: (a) a turnaround sheet (asset workflow), (b) a timed subject turn written into the prompt (quarter/half/full, with direction), (c) a camera orbit (belongs in the camera block). Our facing enum (left/right/camera/away) is enough for the start and end STATE but not for the change between them (a 90-degree and a 180-degree turn both look like left -> right).
Researcher recommendation: keep facing as the state; add an optional per-character turn action {deg: signed int -360..360, sec: float}; keep orbit under camera; lint (own numbers, low confidence): |deg| >= 90 needs sec >= 1.0; |deg| >= 180 needs a turn window of at least 2 s; facing changed between start and end with no turn record = fail.

## C. Other limits the evidence supports
Fast-motion softness: warn when an action beat is labelled fast or running (PGP4 [10:38]-[11:23]; stRsg [16:25]-[17:20]). Shot length: fal.ai says 5 to 15 s at 24 fps per generation (our own rule is 3-7 s). Camera moves beyond a simple push should come from a reference (Blender/storyboard). Within-shot start/end agreement of props and poses (MISTAKES F2/Q5) is not covered by L05 (cross-shot only). L09 entry/exit is untested externally.
