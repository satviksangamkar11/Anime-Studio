Supplied by the owner on 2026-11-04 (from the owner's earlier production bible). Source material: a Hindi kathavachak transcript (00:00:00–00:38:01) plus Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam Canto 10, Chapter 16. ## Owner's format and look
- 5-episode connected short-form anime series, 9:16 vertical, 60–75 s per episode (Ep1 65 s, Ep2 65 s, Ep3 70 s, Ep4 70 s, Ep5 75 s).
- Look: Indian classical cultural soul and staging (in the spirit of the 1993 Indo-Japanese Ramayana film) combined with modern 2.5D prestige action-anime compositing (hard rim light, volumetric light, 3D camera depth). Never name either work in an image or video prompt.
- Language: Hindi / Braj Bhasha dialogue and narration, Sanskrit ślokas where quoted; captions short (max 6–7 words), never repeating the spoken line.
- Tone: devotional and reverent; no clickbait; Kaliya's comedy (EVENT-014) is gentle, tragicomic.

## Three narrative layers to keep visually distinct
1. CANON (ŚB 10.16): the boiling poisoned Kaliya-dah (10.16.4), birds falling from the sky (10.16.4), withered shore and fainting cows (10.16.5), the lone surviving Kadamba tree (10.16.6), Krishna tightening His waist-cloth and slapping His arms like a wrestler (āsphoṭya gāḍha-raśano) and diving in (10.16.6), the Naga-Patnis' vision of Krishna underwater (10.16.9), Kaliya coiling around Krishna (10.16.9), Brajvasis rushing to the bank and Balarama stopping them (10.16.13–22), Krishna expanding His body (10.16.24), dancing on the hoods while Gandharvas play mridanga (10.16.26–30), Kaliya bleeding (10.16.30), the Naga-Patnis with their children and their prayers (10.16.33–53), Kaliya's surrender pleading his nature (10.16.56–58).
2. KATHAVACHAK_VARIATION (Braj oral tradition): Garuda carrying the Amrit Kalash during the Devasura war (Nashik → Prayagraj → Haridwar → resting on this Yamuna Kadamba, drops of Amrit making the tree immortal) (04:35–07:38); the ball game with Balarama absent, Krishna striking Shridama's ball into the Yamuna and putting Maya into Shridama's heart so he demands that exact ball, and Shridama snapping out of it in terror when Krishna climbs the tree (07:38–10:48); Kaliya's first strike touching the tip of Krishna's toe, which Krishna takes as a pranama so He spares him (15:55–16:26); Krishna holding all the tongues like chariot reins and striking only with His heel (एड़ी), never His soft sole (19:16–20:12); Kaliya's whisper to his wives "थोड़ा बहुत जो कृपा मुझे दे रहे हो इनको और दे दो" (33:48); the wives' argument that Krishna's honour (लाज) will suffer if people say "Kaliya died because Krishna hovered over his head" (34:17–35:13); the closing triad of Kali (Kaliya), Bali (Vali) and Bali (King Bali), before whom the Lord accepted defeat (38:01).
3. COMMENTARY: Yamuna as pure bhakti and Kaliya as subtle ahankara (the ego "I am a devotee"), with the Narada Bhakti Sutra (01:59–04:35); the bhakta (one who strives to remember) vs the prapanna (one who never forgets) (28:24–32:07).

## Events (owner's ledger)
- EVENT-001 (00:00–01:59, ŚB 10.16.4–5, CANON): Kaliya's venom boils the Yamuna pool; toxic fumes rise; a bird flying overhead falls dead; cows drinking at the bank collapse. Lush Vrindavan → scorched black sand, dead roots, violet-green miasma. Bhava: serene → dread; Krishna's concern. Silent: yes.
- EVENT-002 (01:59–04:35, COMMENTARY): the pure golden-blue Yamuna (bhakti) choked beneath by black coils (ahankara); a river cross-section with a many-eyed silhouette in the abyss. Silent: yes, with brief voice-over.
- EVENT-003 (04:35–07:38, ŚB 10.16.6 + KATHAVACHAK_VARIATION): why one Kadamba survives: Garuda flees with the Amrit Kalash and rests on this tree; golden drops sink into its bark, giving it a golden-green shield against the venom. Silent: yes (flashback montage).
- EVENT-004 (07:38–09:05, KATHAVACHAK_VARIATION): at dawn, with Balarama absent, Krishna leads His friends to the forbidden black-sand bank despite their fear; the boundary line where green grass turns to black ash-sand.
- EVENT-005 (09:05–10:27, KATHAVACHAK_VARIATION): in the ball game Krishna deliberately strikes the ball into the boiling river; a golden pulse of His Maya enters Shridama's chest; Shridama, uncharacteristically furious, demands that exact ball. Shridama: "कन्हैया, मुझको वही गेंद चाहिए! लेकर आ!" Krishna: "मुझको चुनौती मत दे, मैं कूद जाऊँगा!"
- EVENT-006 (10:27–11:11, CANON + KATHAVACHAK_VARIATION): Krishna tightens His black blanket (Kali Kamli) at the waist, slaps His arms like a malla, climbs the Kadamba and leaps into the poisoned pool as Shridama's Maya breaks and the boys run crying "त्राहिमाम्!" Shridama: "नहीं-नहीं लाला, यहाँ मत कूदियो!"
- EVENT-007 (11:41–14:38, ŚB 10.16.9, CANON): underwater in Kaliya's cavern the Naga-Patnis see Krishna: prekṣaṇīya (impossible to look away from), cloud-dark complexion, golden pitambara, Srivatsa mark, lotus feet, enchanting smile. Terror → captivated devotion.
- EVENT-008 (14:38–16:26, KATHAVACHAK_VARIATION + ŚB 10.16.9): Krishna kicks the sleeping Kaliya's hood; the many-hooded serpent rears and strikes; Krishna steps back; the fang grazes only the tip of His big toe; Krishna smiles inwardly: "you touched My feet in pranama — now you are sharanagata, I cannot kill you."
- EVENT-009 (16:53–18:52, ŚB 10.16.13–23, CANON): Kaliya coils around Krishna; above, Nanda, Yashoda and the gopis weep and rush to jump in; Balarama holds them back ("नारायण समो गुणः") and calls down: "कन्हैया! इतनी प्रतीक्षा किस बात की कर रहे हो? इस कालिया को मज़ा क्यों नहीं चखाते!"
- EVENT-010 (18:52–19:45, ŚB 10.16.24 + KATHAVACHAK_VARIATION): on Balarama's signal Krishna expands His body until the coils snap loose; as the tongues lash out He grabs them together like chariot reins and vaults onto the hoods.
- EVENT-011 (19:45–23:39, ŚB 10.16.26–30 + KATHAVACHAK_VARIATION): whichever hood rises, Krishna crushes it with His heel only; His leaps become the Tandava ("तांडव गति मुंडन पर निरत बनवारी... छम-छम-छम चरण धरत"); Devas and Gandharvas play mridanga and shower flowers; Kaliya bleeds from mouths, eyes and nostrils.
- EVENT-012 (23:39–26:38, ŚB 10.16.31–34, CANON): seeing the blood, the Naga-Patnis rush forward, placing their small serpent children in front to awaken His compassion, and bow, declaring the punishment is grace (anugraha).
- EVENT-013 (26:38–33:41, ŚB 10.16.35–37, CANON + COMMENTARY): why it is grace: prapanna devotees reject heaven, sovereignty and liberation for a speck of dust from His feet, for which even Lakshmi did penance — and Kaliya has the very feet on his head. Partly needs dialogue/subtitles.
- EVENT-014 (33:41–35:13, KATHAVACHAK_VARIATION): exhausted Kaliya weakly gestures to his wives ("give some of this grace to them too"); they hush him; when Krishna still will not step down, the lead wife says that if Kaliya dies people will say he died because Krishna hovered over his head, and Krishna's लाज will be ruined; Krishna instantly leaps down.
- EVENT-015 (35:13–38:01, ŚB 10.16.56–58 + KATHAVACHAK_VARIATION): Kaliya pleads "स्वभावो दुस्त्यजो नाथ... भवान् हि कारणं तत्र" (You made serpents with venom; had You filled my fangs with amrit I would have made the Yamuna nectar); Krishna smiles, accepting defeat before His third challenger (Kaliya, Vali, King Bali); the Yamuna is purified and Kaliya's hoods carry His footprint seal (protection from Garuda); Krishna rises to the sunlit bank.
